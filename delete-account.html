Delete your TukTuk El Balad account

Option 1 — from inside the app (instant): Open the app → My account → Delete account → confirm. Deletion takes effect immediately: your name, phone number and photo are erased, your phone number is released, and sign-in is disabled.

If the app refuses to delete: your account has money attached to it (wallet balance or dues) — the message shows the exact amount. Settle your balance first, then try again.

Option 2 — if you cannot sign in: Email loay.shehata@gmail.com or message +20 10 2857 4799 from your registered phone number, stating that you want your account deleted. We will complete the deletion within 7 business days and confirm to you.

What is deleted

Permanently erased: your name, phone number, profile photo, vehicle details and documents (driver accounts), and push notification tokens. Sign-in access is disabled immediately. Your phone number is released so you may register again with it if you wish.

Retained anonymised: ride history (timestamps and fares only) for accounting and abuse-prevention purposes. It contains no name or phone number and cannot be linked back to you.

Retention period after deletion

None. Once deletion completes, your identifying data is gone from our systems.
